Every Research Engagement Includes

  • Evaluation of your research objective
  • A tailored research plan
  • Research in original record sources
  • Analysis and correlation of evidence
  • A professionally written report
  • Complete source citations
  • Detailed research log
  • Digital copies of records (when available)
  • Recommendations for future research

Research time includes planning, travel to repositories, record retrieval, evidence analysis, report writing, and citation preparation.

Research Expectations

Every project is unique. Because historical records vary by time, place, and survival, no specific result can be guaranteed. Every engagement provides a clear account of the research performed, the evidence located, and the conclusions that can reasonably be supported.
